# Rescheduling an Appointment

## What this assistant can do

Unlike cancellations, the e-Channelling assistant **can process a reschedule
directly** through this chat. You do not need to use the website or call centre.

## Rules and limits

- A booking may be rescheduled **once only**. If you have already rescheduled this
  booking, you must cancel and make a new booking instead.
- Rescheduling must be requested **at least 6 hours** before the original session
  start time. Within 6 hours, contact the hospital directly.
- You can only reschedule to a **different session with the same consultant**.
  Rescheduling to a different doctor is not supported — cancel and rebook instead.
- The new session must have available slots.

## Fee adjustment

If the new session carries a different consultation fee than the original (rare,
since the fee is per consultant), the difference is either:
- Charged to the original payment method (if the new fee is higher), or
- Refunded to the original payment method within 2–5 working days (if lower).

## If your consultant cancels a session

If the doctor cancels the session, all affected patients are notified by SMS and
email. You will be offered rescheduling at no charge, or a full refund if no
suitable alternative session is available. Contact e-Channelling customer support in that case.

## How to reschedule through this chat

1. Tell the assistant you want to reschedule and provide your booking reference
   (e.g. ECH12345678) or registered phone number.
2. The assistant will confirm your current booking details.
3. The assistant will show available sessions for the same consultant.
4. Choose a session. The assistant will summarise the change (new date, time,
   appointment number, any fee adjustment) and ask for your confirmation.
5. Once you confirm, the change is processed immediately.
