# Charges and Fees

The total amount payable for a channelling appointment is made up of three parts.

- Doctor Fee, set by the consultant and shown on the booking screen.
- Hospital Fee, set by the hospital or channelling centre.
- Channelling Service Charge of Rs. 200.00 per appointment.

Additional service charges:

- 1225 telephone service call charge: Rs. 1.50 per call.
- No Show Charge: Rs. 250.00 per appointment, inclusive of taxes.
- Payment gateway charges are absorbed by e-Channelling and are not passed to the patient.

The e-Channelling service on the SLT telephone network is available by dialling the
short code 1225. It is available to SLT Megaline, Smartline, 4G/LTE and Citylink
Postpaid customers.
