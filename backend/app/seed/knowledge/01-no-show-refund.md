# No Show Refund Policy

This policy covers what happens if you miss your appointment, do not show up,
forget to attend, or are unable to attend a session you have already paid for.

If you miss an appointment, you can claim a No Show Refund.

- The No Show Charge is Rs. 250.00 per appointment, inclusive of taxes.
- The refund covers the Doctor Fee, the Hospital Fee and the Channelling Fee.
- You must submit the claim before the session start time.
- Refunds are processed within two (2) working days of an accepted claim.
- Claims submitted after the session start time are not accepted, and no refund
  is payable for that appointment.

To claim, contact e-Channelling customer support with your booking reference
before the session begins.
