# Cancellation Policy

## What this assistant can and cannot do

This assistant can explain the cancellation policy and check your booking details,
but it **cannot process a cancellation on your behalf**. There is no way to cancel
through this chat. To cancel you must use one of the self-service channels below.

## How to cancel

To cancel, contact e-Channelling customer support by phone, email, or through the
website. The phone number, email address, website URL and support hours are listed in
the Customer Support policy.

## Time limits for cancellation

Cancellation is permitted up to **4 hours before** the session start time.

Within the 4-hour window, cancellation is locked. If you miss the window you should
use the No Show Refund process after the session completes.

## Refund amounts

| When you cancel                | Refund you receive                              |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| More than 24 hours before      | Full amount paid, less the channelling service charge (Rs. 200) |
| Between 4 and 24 hours before  | 75% of the total amount paid                    |
| Less than 4 hours before       | Cancellation not permitted; use No Show Refund  |

Refunds are credited to the original payment method within 2 to 5 working days
depending on the bank.

## What happens to the appointment slot

A cancelled appointment number is released back to the session immediately and may
be reallocated to another patient. Once cancelled it cannot be reinstated.
