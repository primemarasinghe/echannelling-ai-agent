# Payment and Refund Support

Accepted payment methods:

- Visa and Mastercard credit or debit cards
- SLT telephone bill, for bookings made through the 1225 service
- eZ Cash and mobile wallets
- Bank transfer for corporate accounts

Payment states:

- Pending: payment initiated but not yet confirmed by the gateway. A pending
  payment holds the appointment number for 15 minutes.
- Successful: the booking is confirmed and a reference is issued.
- Failed: the appointment number is released and no charge is made. If a charge
  appears on the statement for a failed payment it is normally reversed
  automatically within 3 working days.
